FAQ

Common OEM questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for OEM products?

MOQ depends on the product category and level of customisation. Private-label orders on standard products typically start lower than fully custom products. Share your requirement and we will confirm the MOQ in our quotation.

Can you manufacture to our drawings and standards?

Yes. We manufacture to customer drawings, samples or the standard you specify. Our technical team reviews every specification for feasibility before quoting.

Will you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes. Pre-production samples and artwork proofs are provided for your approval. Bulk production starts only after written sample approval.

What are your typical lead times?

Lead time depends on the product, quantity and customisation. Standard private-label orders are faster than new developments. Confirmed lead times are stated in every quotation.

Is our brand, artwork and design kept confidential?

Yes. We are happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Your artwork, drawings and branding are used only for your orders.

Which documents and certificates are supplied?

Standard export documents are supplied with every shipment. Product certificates, calibration certificates and test reports can be supplied as agreed in the order.
